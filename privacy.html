Privacy Policy

Last updated August 7, 2026

What this service is

This website includes a private, owner-operated WhatsApp assistant. It answers questions and can summarize selected progress information stored for the website owner.

Information processed

  • WhatsApp account and message metadata needed to receive and send messages.
  • Message text sent to the assistant and its responses.
  • Selected website progress records requested by the owner, such as study, quiz, and workout summaries.
  • Technical and security logs needed to operate and protect the service.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the owner, answer requests, provide opted-in reminders, retrieve requested website progress, prevent abuse, and maintain the service. Personal information is not sold or used for advertising.

Service providers

Processing may involve Meta and WhatsApp for messaging, Supabase for secure application infrastructure and storage, and an AI model provider for generating responses. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and security

Records are kept only as long as needed to operate the assistant, maintain requested history, and protect the service. Access is restricted to the authorized owner number, and webhook requests are cryptographically verified. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Data deletion

The authorized owner can send forget chat to the WhatsApp assistant to delete saved conversation history associated with the owner. The owner may also request deletion through the website owner. Required security and operational records may remain for a limited period.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the service changes. The date at the top identifies the latest version.
