    Pronunciation

    zh · ch · sh · r

    Say each syllable aloud and keep the tongue curled slightly back for the retroflex initials.

    ui = uei

    After an initial, uei is written ui. In shuì, the tone mark sits on i.

    Neutral tone

    No tone mark; say it lightly. Compare māma and bàba.

    -ng ≠ -n

    Keep the final ng in 生日 · shēngrì.

    ch ≠ sh

    ch starts with a stronger burst of air; sh keeps steady friction.

    现在 ≠ 先

    xiànzài means “now”; xiān means “first.”

    谁 · 几岁

    shéi / shuí asks “who”; jǐ suì asks “how old.”
